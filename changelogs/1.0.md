# KubeThaum 1.0

The first release connects KubeJS scripts to Thaumaturge's native research system.

## Added

- Create, edit, replace, and copy research entries.
- Custom Thaumonomicon tabs and dependencies between research entries.
- Stage text, item and crafting requirements, knowledge, research notes,
  warp, and additional pages.
- Research requirements for supported Thaumaturge recipes.
- JavaScript examples and research guides in Russian and English.
- Optional TypeScript declarations for editor autocomplete.
