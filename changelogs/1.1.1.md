# KubeThaum 1.1.1

Editing built-in research no longer stops the world from loading.

## Fixed

- Editing a built-in research entry that requires a Thaumaturge progress flag,
  such as `thaumaturge:f_onfire` or `thaumaturge:scanned/...`, no longer fails
  with an unknown ID error. On 26.1.2 the world would hang on "preparing for
  world creation". Flags already used by the loaded research are accepted in
  `requireResearch` and `parent`; other unknown IDs are still reported.
