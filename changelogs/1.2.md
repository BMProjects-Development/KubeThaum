# KubeThaum 1.2

## Minecraft 1.21.1

- Ported to NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.1.
- Published separately as `v1.2-mc1.21.1`; existing releases for other
  Minecraft versions are preserved.
- Requires Thaumaturge built from the
  [tested upstream source](https://github.com/Leclowndu93150/Thaumaturge/tree/90fe443c83cc0ce868ec7e57dc944c99694ce533)
  until its matching version is available on CurseForge. Follow the
  [build instructions](https://github.com/BMProjects-Development/KubeThaum/blob/1.21.1/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  and install Thaumaturge and its dependencies separately; they are not bundled.

Require things the player does or scans in the world, and edit built-in
research that already does so.

## Added

- `requireResearch` and `parent` accept Thaumaturge progress flags: scanned
  items, mobs, effects, enchantments and aspects
  (`thaumaturge:scanned/item/minecraft/iron_ingot`), the keys of Thaumaturge
  scan entries, and milestones such as `thaumaturge:m_hellandback`. Items, mobs
  and other subjects that do not exist are still reported as unknown IDs.
- The Thaumonomicon shows the name and icon of a scanned item, mob, effect,
  enchantment or scan entry that research requires, instead of a question mark.
- A guide section on progress flags.

## Fixed

- Editing a built-in research entry that requires a Thaumaturge progress flag,
  such as `thaumaturge:base_auromancy` with `thaumaturge:f_onfire`, no longer
  fails with an unknown ID error. On 26.1.2 the world would hang on "preparing
  for world creation".
