# KubeThaum 1.3

## Compatibility

- Updated to Thaumaturge 1.1.0. KubeThaum 1.3 requires the Thaumaturge 1.1
  series; use KubeThaum 1.2.2 with Thaumaturge 1.0.2.
- Moved scanned-item, creature and effect requirement icons to Thaumaturge's
  new requirement renderer. Their readable tooltips are retained.
- Existing research and recipe scripts keep their API. Thaumaturge's own
  changes to research progression and recipe ingredients still apply.

## Added

- `event.recipes.thaumaturge.runic_augment(displayCatalyst, components,
  perLevel, aspects)` for runic shielding. Supports research gates, aspect
  adjustments and ingredient replacement. Thaumaturge controls eligible items,
  charge increments and the scaling of ingredients, essentia and instability.
- Projectile progress flags `thaumaturge:f_arrow`, `thaumaturge:f_fireball`
  and `thaumaturge:f_spit` are accepted in research requirements and parents,
  including by `/kubethaum check`.
- Updated English and Russian guides and the complete recipe example.

## Upstream review

Thaumaturge 1.1 fixes many gameplay, world generation and book rendering bugs.
Those fixes belong to Thaumaturge; KubeThaum does not need duplicate patches.
Its research builders, recipe schemas, script checking and furnace bonus tools
are still useful and remain available. Thaumaturge still renders some scan
requirements as `?`, so KubeThaum's names and icons are retained. The KubeJS
world-loader compatibility processor addresses a separate NeoForge/KubeJS
integration issue and remains in place.

The book now displays smithing recipes itself. Add ordinary smithing recipes
through KubeJS and reference their IDs with `stage.recipe(...)`; no separate
KubeThaum recipe type is needed. Crystal ingredients follow Thaumaturge's
updated aspect matching rules.

Sources: [official 1.1.0 file](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thaumaturge/files/9428045)
and [upstream changelog](https://gist.github.com/Leclowndu93150/955cb128ed296b02d4cfada706b5e515).

## Development

- Pinned the official Thaumaturge 1.1.0 dependency and centralized its supported
  version range in `gradle.properties`.
- Added checks against the released client renderer and tooltip method
  signatures, so optional mixins cannot silently become stale in the pinned build.
- Expanded native GameTest coverage for runic augmentation and projectile flags.
